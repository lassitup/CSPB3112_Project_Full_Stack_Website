About Daisy A Jewelry



"I'm holding on to pieces of us
That I just can't let go
I know this is a desperate kind of love
But it feels like it's home"
-NFWait

Though there is no collection of words with which I could honor this fierce lady, they would still fail to capture the essence of her soul, the reach of her impact, the legacy she built within her children, grandchildren, beloved family and friends. She chose to use every moment of her life to serve others; the labor of her hands not only fed her own, but provided strength, peace, and love to every life she encountered. June 2019 began my journey in flower creations to honor this amazing lady and cherish her memory and others after her for years to come.

Hello there! My name is Chelsea Lassiter and this is my family ❤️ For many years, I've pondered the thought of turning my dream and passion for preserving moments into a reality and the time has finally come… Welcome to Daisy A Jewelry
